Shopping and Cooking Assistant


Timeline: Sept 2021 - May 2022 (9 months)

Team Size: 2 Members

Tech Stack: Java, MySQL, Android Studio

Link to code (GitHub): Shopping and Cooking Assistant

Technical Overview

Developed a comprehensive mobile solution designed to streamline household management through calorie tracking, inventory management, and automated meal planning.

  • Mobile Development: Engineered a native Android application focusing on user-centric design and intuitive navigation.
  • Cloud Integration: Implemented a real-time online database (Firebase) to sync user pantry inventory and shopping lists across multiple devices.
  • Feature Set:
    • Dynamic calorie tracking and nutritional data logging.
    • Automated meal suggestions based on current pantry "stock."
    • Collaborative shopping lists with real-time updates.
  • Communication: Successfully presented the technical architecture and live demonstration to University of Galway faculty members.
